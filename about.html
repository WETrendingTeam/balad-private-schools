Our Story

At Balad Private Schools, we believe every child has the potential to succeed. Our story is built around the belief that education should develop the whole child, not only prepare them for examinations. Through dedicated teaching, practical learning, innovation, discipline and strong moral values, we create an environment where learners can grow with confidence. From the early years to college, we encourage curiosity, independent thinking, teamwork and a love for learning while helping students develop the knowledge, skills and character they need for life.

Proprietor's Welcome Message

"Raising every Muslim child educationally and morally to build an industrious, just and peaceful society."

Welcome to Balad Private Schools, where every child is valued, nurtured and inspired to achieve excellence. We are committed to providing a safe, disciplined and caring environment that supports academic success, strong moral values and personal growth.

Our goal is to equip every learner with the knowledge, skills and character needed to become confident, responsible and successful individuals. We appreciate the trust parents place in us and remain dedicated to giving every child the best educational experience.

Alhaji Bello Aliyu Alade, MNIM, MCPN
